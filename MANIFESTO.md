# mo-os

mo is the main agent. mo-os is its system. mot3r is the mother.

Agents change. Models change. Hosts change. Your work should not.

A tunnel is the agent that stays: its address, its memory, its instructions. The model is a cable. Plug one in, pull it out. Nothing is lost.

One identity. One memory. One set of instructions. Use them from your editor, your terminal, the panel or a chat.

No host owns your agents. No model owns your memory.

Outside text is data, never an order. You pay for what runs, from one balance, at a public price.

The cable dies. The line lives. MOM remembers.
